# Provenance

The canonical public source is the corrected article “We Asked 8 LLMs to Run Our Family's Life. Two Tried to Book a Vacation.” The reviewed publication snapshot was commit `bd7c06869b4cd9f1bb36efcb2111f35b1d267426`, dated 2026-07-20.

Internal benchmark materials were reviewed to confirm the design counts, correction history, and exclusion boundary. The following were excluded rather than copied:

- the proprietary frozen system prompt;
- the production action schema and full tool catalog;
- all original scenario text and mock product state;
- raw and processed response archives;
- provider logs;
- one malformed processed result file;
- local machine metadata and paths;
- credentials and environment configuration.

The public synthetic corpus, example outputs, and scoring implementation were created specifically for this repository.
